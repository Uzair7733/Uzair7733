## 🚀 About Me

I'm an aerospace engineer pursuing my **MEng at Colorado State University**, working across orbital mechanics, guidance & control, CFD, propulsion, and machine learning. I build simulations, design missions, and translate complex engineering problems into clean, data-driven solutions.

I currently serve as a **Graduate Teaching Assistant for MECH 207**, running weekly lab sessions, supervising hands-on Arduino hardware practicals, and managing full-cycle grading for 20 hours/week. Before that, I was a **Grader for MECH 519 (Orbital Mechanics/STK)**, mentoring 50+ students on Systems Tool Kit trajectory and mission-design assignments.

Before CSU, I spent a year as a **Technical Author at Capgemini (Airbus A220)** — writing and reviewing flight-critical aircraft manuals to 97%+ accuracy standards. That experience gave me a sharp eye for precision and a deep respect for documentation in safety-critical systems.

Right now I'm focused on orbital mechanics & constellation design, GNC for autonomous systems, and low-carbon propulsion chemistry — while wrapping up a dual **MBA in Operations Management**. I'm actively open to opportunities in **commercial aviation, space startups, and hybrid technical-operations roles**.
